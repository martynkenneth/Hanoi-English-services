Gyms in Hanoi: Membership Prices

What Hanoi gyms cost by tier, how much a long contract really saves, and the terms to check before signing.

Prices checked August 2026. Vietnamese đồng is the authoritative figure — dollar equivalents move with the exchange rate.

How much does a gym membership cost in Hanoi?

A basic local gym in Hanoi costs 200,000–500,000₫ a month, a mid-range gym 600,000–1,200,000₫, and a premium gym or club 1,200,000–3,000,000₫ and up. Day passes run 40,000–300,000₫ depending on the venue. The premium end buys pool, sauna, classes and multi-branch access rather than better equipment.

Is a long gym contract cheaper in Hanoi?

Substantially — one Hanoi chain charges 1,200,000₫ for a single month on its entry tier but 8,000,000₫ for twelve months, an effective 667,000₫ a month. Its top tier drops from 3,000,000₫ monthly to an effective 1,500,000₫ on an annual contract, and off-peak annual membership falls to about 583,000₫ a month. The saving is real, but so is the commitment.

What should I check before joining a Hanoi gym?

Check the commitment length, because an attractive per-month figure may require 24 to 60 months paid upfront. Confirm which branches you can access, whether classes, pool and sauna are included, any joining fee, and the rules on freezing or transferring a membership. Displayed prices in Hanoi are frequently negotiable, particularly at the end of a month.

Are there English-speaking gyms in Hanoi?

Yes — several Hanoi gyms and studios, concentrated in Tay Ho and Ba Dinh, have English-speaking trainers and an international membership. Class-based studios and CrossFit boxes tend to be the most English-friendly because coaching requires communication. Every gym in this directory was phoned to confirm.
